## Resolution Standards

Preferred:
- Master video: 3840×2160 (4K)
- UI renders: 3840×2160 or larger
- Hero photography: 4K minimum
- Logos: SVG
- UI transparency assets: PNG/WebP
- Video footage: 4K, 24/30fps

Avoid:
- Upscaling low-resolution screenshots
- Screenshots with visible compression
- Cartoonized family imagery
- Mixed visual styles between scenes
