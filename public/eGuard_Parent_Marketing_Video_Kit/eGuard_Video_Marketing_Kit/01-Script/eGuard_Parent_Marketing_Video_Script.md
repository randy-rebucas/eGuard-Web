# Production Notes

## Visual Ratio
- 60% real family footage/photography
- 30% eGuard UI/product visuals
- 10% brand graphics and transitions

## Avoid
- Cartoon families
- Generic 3D child illustrations
- Frightening cybersecurity imagery
- Surveillance-camera aesthetics
- Dark hacker imagery
- Overly colorful children's-app styling
- Unrealistic device interfaces

## Desired Look
Premium technology commercial, photorealistic, natural skin texture, realistic devices, cinematic depth of field, soft daylight, light-blue accents, clean white surfaces, subtle futuristic motion graphics.

## Core emotional progression

Family life → Parent concern → eGuard → Setup → Verification → Visibility → Reassurance → Family
