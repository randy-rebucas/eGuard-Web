# Products and Services Catalogue

## Summary

The Products and Services Catalogue lists every eGuard product and service sold or offered by DevCom Digital Marketing Services (short: DevCom): the three plans (Free, eGuard Plus and eGuard Family), sponsor codes and organizations for schools, community groups and businesses, the apps and extension that apply protections, and the free help for parents. It is for anyone who describes, sells or supports eGuard and needs the published facts in one place. All prices are in Philippine pesos (₱): Free costs ₱0, eGuard Plus ₱149 a month or ₱1,490 a year, and eGuard Family ₱249 a month or ₱2,490 a year. Plans are bought only on the web at www.eguard.family, only by the family admin, and every plan includes verification and Configuration Health. Internal costs, margins and discount rules are not in this catalogue; they are in "Pricing and Discount Rules".

## How plans are bought and paid for

Every eGuard plan is bought on the website www.eguard.family, in Settings › Subscription, through the payment provider PayMongo. Only the family admin can buy or cancel a plan; other parents in the family see the plan but cannot change it. The eGuard apps show the family's plan but do not sell it, and have no buy button. No card is needed to start on Free.

| Way to pay | How it works | Payment methods |
|---|---|---|
| Pass | One month or one year, paid once. A pass never renews. eGuard sends a reminder 3 days before it ends, and buying another pass adds another period after the current one | GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card |
| Auto-renew | Charged every month or every year until the family admin turns it off. After turning it off, the family keeps the plan until the end of the paid period. If a renewal payment fails, the family keeps access for a 3-day grace period while the payment is retried | Card or Maya |

A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months (two months free). A family uses one way of paying at a time: a pass cannot be bought while auto-renew is on.

**Moving to a smaller plan never removes anything.** Children and devices over the new limit stay protected; the family cannot add more until it is under the limit. If location sharing ends, current locations are cleared and any location history is hidden until the family upgrades again.

## Plan limits and legacy limits

Plan limits are the number of children and devices a family can add. Phones, tablets, browsers and Windows PCs each count as one device.

| Plan | Children | Devices |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | 1 |
| eGuard Plus | 3 | 5 |
| eGuard Family | 6 | 12 |

These limits apply from 2026-10-28. Families that were paying before 2026-10-28 keep their older limits ("legacy limits") for as long as they stay on a paid plan, upgrades included: 5 children and 10 devices on eGuard Plus, 10 children and 20 devices on eGuard Family. A family that returns to Free and later buys again gets the current limits.

## Sponsor codes

Sponsor codes are prepaid eGuard plans that an organization buys and gives to families.

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | A code that gives one family a paid plan (eGuard Plus or eGuard Family) for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. An organization buys codes in batches and hands them out |
| Who it is for | Organizations paying for families' plans, for example a barangay funding families who cannot pay, or an employer offering eGuard as a benefit |
| Included | 1 to 200 codes per batch, paid once through PayMongo (GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card); each code can be redeemed within 12 months of payment. The family admin redeems a code on the website in Settings › Subscription ("Have a sponsor code?"); the plan shows as "Sponsored plan" and a reminder is sent 3 days before it ends. A code works like a gift card: a family does not need to join the organization to redeem it |
| Not included | Redeeming in the mobile apps. The organization never sees which family redeemed a code |
| Availability | Available now on the web |
| Public list price | Months × the plan's monthly web price × number of codes. Larger batches get a published volume discount of 10% (10–49 codes), 15% (50–99 codes) or 20% (100–200 codes); the rules are in "Pricing and Discount Rules" |
| Related products | Organizations, eGuard Plus, eGuard Family |
