# Customer Support Playbook

## Summary

The Customer Support Playbook describes how DevCom Digital Marketing Services supports eGuard customers: the channels parents and organizations use, how requests are prioritized, handled, handed over and closed, and the standard resolution for the issues seen most often. It is written for the staff who answer the support inbox, Messenger and support tickets, and for anyone who takes over a request from them. The published promise is that support replies within 1 business day; there are no approved resolution targets, refund limits or approvers yet, and those are marked [TO CONFIRM]. Staff work from the console (staff back office), which shows accounts, plans, purchases and tickets but never a child's activity. Prices are not repeated here: they live in "Products and Services Catalogue" and "Pricing and Discount Rules".

## How to set priority

Support priority for eGuard requests is not defined in the sources. The examples below sort real request types from the sources into the proposed levels; the levels themselves are [TO CONFIRM: priority levels and the rule for assigning them].

| Proposed priority | Examples of requests |
|---|---|
| Urgent | A parent reports a child in danger, grooming or exploitation; a parent believes someone else is signed in to their account; a payment was taken twice |
| High | A parent paid but the plan did not change; the payment page says online payment is not available; an organization's paid batch shows no codes; an alert says eGuard was removed or tampered with and the parent cannot restore it |
| Normal | A device shows as offline; a setting shows Failed; a pairing code does not work; a sponsor code is refused; a parent cannot add a child or device because the plan is full |
| Low | Questions about plans, features, iPhone or Windows availability; requests for guides; feedback |

When a request fits more than one row, staff use the higher priority.

## Standard procedure for a new request

The support team handles a new eGuard request in these steps.

1. **Read the request in full.** Note the channel, the ticket category (if any) and what the customer is asking for.
2. **Confirm who is writing.** The method for confirming that the sender owns the account is [TO CONFIRM: identity check before discussing an account]. Staff do not discuss an account with someone whose email does not match a parent on it until this is settled.
3. **Look up the family in the console.** Search by parent email or name, family name or id. Every search and every page opened is recorded in the staff audit log.
4. **Check the facts the console shows.** Plan and how it is paid ("renews" or "ends"), purchases, parents (verified email, sign-in method, two-step on or off, last active), number of children, devices and browsers, tickets, organizations joined and sponsor codes redeemed.
5. **Do not ask for or look for a child's activity.** The console does not show it (see "What staff can and cannot do in the console"), and staff do not ask parents to send it.
6. **Find the matching issue** in the "Common issue" sections below, or in the Help Center.
7. **Reply from the support inbox.** Tickets cannot be answered from the console. Write in plain language and give the exact screen path (for example "Settings › Subscription").
8. **Hand over** when a trigger in "When to hand over to a person" applies.
9. **Close the request** as described in "Closing a request".

## Refunds, credits and exceptions

Refunds for eGuard purchases are made in the PayMongo dashboard; the full steps are in "Refunding a Purchase". What a refund does:

| Purchase | Effect of a refund |
|---|---|
| Pass | The pass ends at once and the family's plan is recalculated |
| Auto-renew invoice | Not matched automatically. Staff refund in PayMongo and cancel the subscription; the plan then ends at the end of the paid period. Ending it at once needs an engineering database change |
| Sponsor-code batch | Unredeemed codes are cancelled. Plans already redeemed keep running. Partial refunds are handled by support |
| Sponsor code cancelled by the organization | No refund, and the cancellation cannot be undone |

## Common issue: a device shows as offline

A device is offline when it has not synced with eGuard for more than a day (24 hours). The standard resolution:

1. Tell the parent that protection stays on: the device keeps following the last rules it received, and the device page shows what it last reported.
2. Ask the parent to check that the device is charged, connected to the internet, and that eGuard is not restricted by battery saver.
3. Explain that once the device reconnects, eGuard verifies every protection again automatically, and picks up changes, usually within about 5 minutes.
4. Note that "Run Configuration Check" is unavailable while the device is offline.

A browser that has not checked in for a day shows "Not seen for a day". A browser shown as "Disconnected for security" had its sign-in key used from two places; the parent removes it and adds it again.

## Common issue: a setting shows Failed or is not verified

A setting shows **Failed** when the child's device reported something other than what the parent set. The parent sees what the device reported, and gets an alert. The standard resolution:

1. Ask the parent to open the protection and use **Fix this**, which reopens the setup for that child.
2. If the device is offline, the change waits for it ("Waiting for the device to come online"). Follow the offline issue above.
3. If the setting needs steps on the device ("Waiting for you to finish the steps"), the parent completes the guided setup on the child's device.
4. A change that no device confirms within 7 days is cancelled. The parent gets a notice that the change was not confirmed; the earlier setting stays in place, and the parent tries again once the device is online.
5. Send the Help Center article on settings that are not verified.

## Common issue: the parent paid but the plan has not changed

A payment that does not show at once is usually still being confirmed by PayMongo. The standard resolution:

1. Ask the parent to reload Settings › Subscription. eGuard re-checks an unfinished payment about every minute.
2. In the console, check the family's purchases and whether the plan now shows.
3. A checkout that was never paid closes on its own after 24 hours; nothing is charged.
4. If the parent still sees "waiting" after reloading, hand the request to engineering with the family id and the time of payment.

## Common issue: a payment or card is refused

Payment errors on eGuard come either from the card or e-wallet, or from the payment setup. The standard resolution:

1. **A card error during auto-renew:** the page shows PayMongo's own message, such as an invalid or declined card. Suggest another card, Maya, or a pass, which also accepts GCash and QR Ph.
2. **"PayMongo refused the request"** or **"online payment isn't available":** this is a setup problem on eGuard's side. Apologize and hand over to engineering at once.
3. **A large sponsor-code batch with GCash:** e-wallets cap single payments; suggest card or QR Ph.

## Common issue: auto-renew, renewals and changing how a plan is paid

Questions about auto-renew have standard answers:

| Question | Standard answer |
|---|---|
| "I turned auto-renew off but I'm still on the paid plan" | Expected. The plan stays until the end of the paid period; the page shows "Ends on …" |
| "My renewal failed" | The page shows "Renewal failed". PayMongo retries once a day, three times, and the plan stays for 3 days of grace. If the retries fail, the family returns to Free |
| "I want to change my card" | Not available yet. Turn auto-renew off and start it again after the paid period, or buy passes |
| "I want to switch between monthly and yearly" | Turn auto-renew off and start the other period once the paid time ends |
| "I want auto-renew but I have a pass" | Auto-renew can start only after the pass's paid time ends, to avoid paying twice |
| "I want a receipt" | PayMongo emails receipts for passes; auto-renew invoices follow PayMongo's email settings. eGuard itself issues no receipts |

## Common issue: the parent cannot buy or upgrade in the app

Plans for eGuard are bought only on the web, in Settings › Subscription, and only by the family admin. The apps show the plan but do not sell it and do not link to the website, because app store payment rules forbid steering users to outside payment. Other parents in the family see "Only the family admin can change the plan." The standard resolution is to explain that the family admin buys on the parent web dashboard.

## Common issue: the plan is full and a child or device cannot be added

eGuard enforces a limit of children and devices per plan. Phones, tablets, computers and browsers each count as one device. The current limits are in "Products and Services Catalogue"; families who were paying before 2026-10-28 keep their legacy limits while they stay subscribed. Whether a browser on a paired Windows PC takes a device slot is [TO CONFIRM: whether a browser on a paired Windows PC takes a device slot].

The standard resolution:

1. Check in the console how many children, devices and browsers the family has.
2. Explain that the parent can remove a device, or the family admin can move to a larger plan.
3. If the family moved to a smaller plan, reassure the parent: nothing is removed. Children and devices over the limit stay protected; only adding more is blocked.

## Common issue: a pairing code does not work

eGuard pairing codes are 8 characters and expire after 15 minutes. The standard resolution:

1. Ask the parent to get a new code and enter it before it expires. Spaces, dashes and lower case are accepted.
2. If the screen says the code was replaced, another parent or tab made a newer code; use the newest one.
3. Only a parent with a verified email can make pairing codes. Ask the parent to verify their email first.
4. A phone code works only in the eGuard app; a browser code works only in the eGuard browser extension.
5. If the plan is full, follow the "plan is full" issue above.

## Common issue: a sponsor code is refused

A sponsor code gives a family a paid plan for a set number of months. It is redeemed only on the web, by the family admin, at Settings › Subscription › "Have a sponsor code?"; it cannot be redeemed in the apps. A code is refused when:

| Reason | What to tell the family |
|---|---|
| Already redeemed, cancelled by the organization, or past its redeem-by date (12 months after the batch was paid) | Ask the organization for another code |
| Auto-renew is on | Turn auto-renew off first; the code's months start when the paid time ends |
| The family has paid time on a different plan | Redeem after that paid time ends |
| The family's plan is billed through Google Play | The code cannot be used while that plan runs |

An organization can check any of its codes on its organization page, or through the Organization API.

## Common issue: an organization's API key stops working

The eGuard Organization API lets an organization's own systems read its codes and counts. A key stops working in these cases:

| Response | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | The key was revoked, or the admin who created it stopped managing the organization or deleted their account | Create a new key |
| 403 "plan_required" (shown as Paused) | The creating admin's family is no longer on eGuard Family | The key works again when that family returns to eGuard Family |
| 403 "forbidden" | A read-only key tried to cancel a code | Create a key with "Read and cancel codes" access |
| 429 | Too many requests (600 per 10 minutes per key) or too many wrong keys from one address | Wait and retry |

A key is shown only once. A lost key cannot be shown again: the admin revokes it and creates another. An organization can have 5 working keys.

## Common issue: deleting an account or exporting data

Parents can export their family's data and delete their account themselves, from Settings on the web or in the app. The standard answers:

1. **Export:** any parent can download the family's data as a file. Suggest exporting before deleting.
2. **Delete:** the family admin deleting their account deletes the whole family (children, devices, history and the other parents). Another parent deleting their account removes only themselves. Deleting asks for the password, or typing DELETE for accounts without one.
3. **Cannot sign in:** send the parent to www.eguard.family/delete-account, which explains the email request. Hand the request over as described above.

## Common issue: emails from eGuard

Parents sometimes ask why they received an email or how to stop one. eGuard sends two kinds of broadcast:

- **Product updates** (news and new features) go only to parents who have product updates turned on. The parent can turn them off from the email's unsubscribe link, or in Settings › Notifications.
- **Service notices** (terms or privacy changes, security notices, outages, account changes) go to every verified parent and cannot be unsubscribed.

People on the public mailing lists (eGuard news, iPhone launch) can unsubscribe from the footer link, which deletes their address from every list.

## Closing a request

The support team closes an eGuard request when the customer has a complete answer or the hand-over owner has confirmed the outcome.

1. Make sure the final reply went out from the support inbox.
2. For a ticket, open it in the console and press **Close**. The change is recorded in the staff audit log, and the parent sees the ticket as Closed in the app or on Settings › Support.
3. If the customer writes again about the same problem, **Reopen** the ticket rather than starting a new one.
4. For Messenger and plain email, record the outcome where the team tracks requests [TO CONFIRM: where Messenger and email requests are logged].
