# Customer FAQ

## Summary

The Customer FAQ answers the questions parents, guardians and organizations ask about eGuard, the parental control product of DevCom Digital Marketing Services, using only facts that are safe to tell a customer. It is for the support team (and sales, where useful) to quote in replies, on Messenger and on social media. The most important facts: eGuard marks a setting Verified only after the child's device confirms it; Free covers 1 child and 1 device; plans are bought on the web by the family admin; and eGuard never reads messages, photos or browsing content. Prices must be checked against www.eguard.family/pricing before quoting.

## What is eGuard?

eGuard is a parental control app for Filipino families, made by DevCom Digital Marketing Services in Baybay City, Leyte, Philippines. Parents set rules for their children's devices, such as screen time, bedtime and which apps they can use. eGuard then checks that those rules are really running on each device.

## How is eGuard different from other parental control apps?

eGuard differs from most parental control apps because it does not stop at "Saved". eGuard sends each setting to the child's device, and the device reports back what it actually has. The two results a parent sees are explained in "What do Verified and Failed mean?".

## What do Verified and Failed mean?

Verified means the child's device has confirmed it has the setting the parent chose. Failed means the device reported something other than what the parent set; the parent sees what the device reported and can fix it from the same screen. eGuard checks again every time the device syncs.

## What is Configuration Health?

Configuration Health is one score per child across eGuard's 10 protections. It shows how much of what the parent set up is really in place on the child's devices. It measures the devices' settings, never the child's behavior. A protection that a device's platform does not allow is never counted against the score.

## How does eGuard work?

eGuard works in four steps. The parent creates a family account and adds a child, choosing a profile (Protected, Balanced or Custom) so eGuard can suggest settings for the child's age. The parent pairs the child's device with a one-time code and switches on the protections they choose. eGuard keeps checking that each protection is still applied, and alerts the parent if something changes.

## How long does setup take?

Setting up eGuard takes a few minutes for a child's Android phone. The parent installs eGuard on the phone, connects it to the family account with a pairing code, and switches on the protections they choose. A pairing code works for 15 minutes; if it runs out, the parent gets a new one.

## Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card is needed to start eGuard. The Free plan needs no card.

## Which devices does eGuard work on?

eGuard works on Android phones and tablets through the eGuard app on Google Play [TO CONFIRM: whether eGuard for Android is live on Google Play], and on Chrome, Edge and Firefox through the eGuard browser extension. Parents manage all of them from one parent web dashboard.

## Does eGuard work on iPhone and iPad?

eGuard does not work on iPhone and iPad yet. The iPhone and iPad app is coming soon. Parents can sign up at www.eguard.family/iphone to get an email when it is out.

## Does eGuard work on Windows PCs?

eGuard does not work on Windows PCs yet. eGuard for Windows is being tested, and there is no release date. It will let parents block and limit PC games and apps from the same dashboard, and it never touches a game from the inside, so it is safe with anti-cheat.

## What happens when my child's phone is offline?

Protection stays on when a child's phone is offline. The device keeps following the last rules it received, and picks up any changes as soon as it reconnects, usually within about 5 minutes. Once it reconnects, eGuard verifies every protection again automatically.

## What can I control with eGuard?

eGuard covers 10 protections: Screen Time (a daily limit, with a separate weekend limit), Bedtime, App Restrictions (age ratings, per-app limits and blocking), App Approval, Content Restrictions (age ratings for movies, TV and books), Web Filtering, Downloads, Location, Notification Controls and Uninstall Protection. eGuard Plus and eGuard Family add location sharing and category limits, such as one "gaming time" limit across all games.

## What does the eGuard browser extension do?

The eGuard browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox blocks harmful sites and categories and turns on SafeSearch. When a site is blocked, the child can ask a parent to open it for 15 minutes, an hour, today or always. Every rule set the extension receives is digitally signed, so it only follows rules that really came from eGuard.

## Can my child switch eGuard off or uninstall it?

Uninstall Protection keeps eGuard from being removed without the parent. If a setting is changed on the device anyway, the device reports what it really has, so eGuard shows it as Failed and sends the parent an alert naming the device and the setting.

## How will I know if something stops working?

eGuard alerts parents when a protection is turned off, a device stops checking in, a limit is reached, a new app is installed or an app is waiting for approval. Every plan gets email and in-app alerts, and push alerts for security alerts (eGuard removed, tampered with or turned off). eGuard Plus and eGuard Family push every alert [TO CONFIRM: whether push alerts work], and add alerts when a setting changes on the child's device. On Free, a parent still sees every change in the child's history.

## Will my child know eGuard is on?

Children always know eGuard is on: eGuard is never hidden. A child can open it and see which rules are on and how much screen time is left. On Android it shows a notification while it is active. There is a page that explains eGuard to children at www.eguard.family/for-kids.

## What can I see, and what stays private?

Parents see whether each protection is on, screen-time totals, the names of apps used, and location only if the family turns it on. eGuard never sees messages, photos, calls, what the child types, or which websites the child visits, beyond a site the child asks the parent to unblock. The browser extension reports only how many pages it blocked, never which sites.

## How is my family's data protected?

eGuard collects only what it needs to verify the family's protections. It shows no ads and never sells children's data. The privacy policy is written under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and is at www.eguard.family/privacy.

## How long does eGuard keep my family's data?

eGuard deletes activity automatically after 90 days. For location, only the current location is kept; location history is off unless the family turns it on.

## Can eGuard staff see my child's activity?

eGuard staff cannot see a child's activity. Staff see accounts (plans, purchases, parents' email addresses and how many children and devices a family has), never a child's location, browsing, app use, screen time, alerts or photos.

## What counts as a device?

A phone, a tablet, a computer and a browser each count as one device on an eGuard plan. Whether a browser on a Windows PC that eGuard already protects takes its own device slot is [TO CONFIRM: whether a browser on a paired Windows PC takes a device slot].

## Is verification included on the Free plan?

Verification is included on every eGuard plan, Free included. Every plan checks each protection on the device and shows Configuration Health. Paying adds capacity and features, not honesty.

## Is paying yearly cheaper?

Paying yearly for eGuard is cheaper. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months, so two months are free.

## How can I pay?

eGuard accepts GCash, Maya, QR Ph or a card. A pass can be paid with any of these. Auto-renew is paid with a card or Maya.

## What's the difference between a pass and auto-renew?

A pass is one month or one year of eGuard, paid once, and it never renews. eGuard sends a reminder 3 days before a pass ends, and buying another pass adds to the time left. Auto-renew charges the family every month or every year until it is turned off; the payment methods for each are in "How can I pay?".

## Can I pay in the app?

Plans cannot be bought in the eGuard app. Plans are bought on the web, in Settings › Subscription, and the app shows the plan as soon as it is paid.

## Who in my family can buy or cancel a plan?

Only the family admin can buy or cancel an eGuard plan. The subscription belongs to the family, not to one parent. Other parents in the family see the plan and the message "Only the family admin can change the plan."

## Can more than one parent use the account?

More than one parent can use the same eGuard family account on every plan, Free included. A second parent can be added to share the work.

## What happens if I turn off auto-renew?

Turning off auto-renew keeps the plan until the end of the period already paid for. The subscription page then shows "Ends on" with the date.

## What happens if a renewal payment fails?

A failed renewal does not end the plan at once. The page shows "Renewal failed", the payment is retried, and the plan stays for 3 more days. If the payment still does not go through, the family returns to Free.

## Can I change my card or switch between monthly and yearly?

Changing the card for auto-renew is not available yet. A parent with an expiring card can turn auto-renew off and on again after the paid period, or buy passes instead. To switch between monthly and yearly, the family admin turns auto-renew off and starts the other one once the paid time ends.

## What happens if I move to a smaller plan?

Nothing is removed when a family moves to a smaller eGuard plan. Children and devices over the new limit stay protected; the family just cannot add more until it is under the limit. If location sharing ends, current locations are cleared and any location history is hidden until the family upgrades again.

## I subscribed before 28 October 2026. Do my limits change?

Families who were paying for eGuard Plus or eGuard Family before 28 October 2026 keep their earlier limits for as long as they stay subscribed: eGuard Plus up to 5 children and 10 devices, and eGuard Family up to 10 children and 20 devices. Moving from eGuard Plus to eGuard Family keeps the larger limits too. If the subscription ends and the family subscribes again later, the current limits apply.

## Is Family Pro the same as eGuard Family?

Family Pro is the old name of eGuard Family. It is the same plan at the same price.

## Can schools or organizations use eGuard?

Schools, community groups and businesses can use eGuard through an organization. Any parent with a verified email can create an organization, share its join code with families, and buy sponsor codes that pay for families' plans. What an organization can see is explained in "What does an organization see if my family joins?". More information is at www.eguard.family/for-schools.

## What does an organization see if my family joins?

An organization sees only that a family joined, counted together with other families. It never sees the family's name, children, devices, settings, activity or location, and never sees which family redeemed a code. The family admin can leave an organization at any time.

## How do I use a sponsor code?

The family admin redeems an eGuard sponsor code on the web, in Settings › Subscription › "Have a sponsor code?". The family then gets the plan for the code's months. A code must be redeemed within 12 months of the organization's purchase. A code cannot be redeemed while auto-renew is on (turn it off first), or while the family has paid time on a different plan.

## What do sponsor codes cost an organization?

Sponsor codes give a family eGuard Plus or eGuard Family for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. An organization buys 1 to 200 codes per batch. Larger batches cost less per code: 10–49 codes get 10% off, 50–99 get 15% off, and 100–200 get 20% off. The organization pays online with GCash, Maya, QR Ph or a card.

## How do I export or delete my family's data?

Parents can export or delete their family's data at any time from Settings. Deleting the family admin's account deletes the whole family, including children, devices, history and other parents; another parent deleting their account removes only themselves. Steps, including a request by email for people who cannot sign in, are at www.eguard.family/delete-account.

## How do I stop eGuard's news emails?

eGuard's news emails can be stopped with the unsubscribe link in any of them, or by turning off Product updates in Settings › Notifications. Notices about the account, privacy or security are still sent, because they are not marketing.

## Where can I learn more about keeping my kids safe online?

The eGuard Knowledge Center at www.eguard.family/learn has 100 free guides on screen time, online safety, cyberbullying and popular apps. Setup and troubleshooting articles are in the Help Center at www.eguard.family/help.

## How do I contact eGuard?

eGuard support is at support@devcomdigital.com, or by Messenger on the eGuard Facebook Page. Parents can also send a request from Contact Support in the app or Settings › Support on the web. Support replies within 1 business day.
