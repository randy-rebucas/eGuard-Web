## Summary

26 documents are drafted from the 32 source files in `docs/`: 15 rated Draft (enough material) and 11 rated Thin (many gaps). Seven catalogue documents were skipped. All 26 drafts are version 0.1 and together hold 687 [TO CONFIRM] markers. Their Open questions sections hold 357 items. The M7 final check made 137 fixes and the M5 contradiction check made 24 more edits across 10 drafts. 32 contradictions that come from the sources are listed, not resolved, and 8 drafts have split proposals. No draft is ready to upload: each one still has [TO CONFIRM] markers and an Open questions section, and the questions that block the most documents are the owner role and dates (all 26), the current plan limits (15) and whether push alerts work (15).
