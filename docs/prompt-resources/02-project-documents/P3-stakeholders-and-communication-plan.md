# P3 Stakeholders and communication plan

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Project | Internal | Project (objective) |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "<Project name>: Stakeholders
and Communication Plan". Sections: Summary; Stakeholders (table: Stakeholder
(role or organisation) | Interest | Influence | What they need from the project);
Who decides what (table: Decision | Decides | Consulted); Regular updates
(table: Update | Audience | Frequency | Channel | Prepared by); Escalation
(when an issue goes to which role, and how fast); Customer or partner contact
rules (who may contact them and through which channel); Document control;
Open questions. Use role titles and channel names, not personal phone numbers
or private email addresses.
```
