# D1 Customer support playbook

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| SOP (controlled) | Internal | Department: support |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write the SOP "Customer Support
Playbook". Sections: Summary; Support channels and hours; Response and
resolution targets (table by priority); How to set priority (with examples);
Standard procedure for a new request (numbered steps); Refunds, credits and
exceptions (limits and who approves each); When to hand over to a person
(specific triggers); Common issues and their standard resolution (one "## "
section per issue, titled with the issue); Closing a request; Document control;
Open questions.
```
