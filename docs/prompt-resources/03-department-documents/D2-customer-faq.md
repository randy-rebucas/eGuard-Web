# D2 Customer FAQ

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Customer support | Public or Internal | Department: support (add sales if useful) |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Customer FAQ". Each question
is its own "## " section, written the way a customer would ask it, and the answer
starts by restating the subject (for example "## Can I change my plan mid-month?"
then "Customers can change their plan..."). Answers are 2-6 sentences and use only
facts from my material. Group nothing; one question per section. Include only
information that is safe to tell a customer. Then Document control and Open
questions (including questions customers are likely to ask that my material
cannot answer).
```
