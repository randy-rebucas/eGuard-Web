# D3 Sales playbook

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & marketing | Internal | Department: sales |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Sales Playbook". Sections:
Summary; Ideal customer profile (industries, size, signals of a good fit and a
poor fit); Buyer roles and what each cares about; Sales stages (table: Stage |
Entry criteria | Activities | Exit criteria | System where it is recorded);
Qualification questions; Value messages by customer type; Common objections
(one "## " section per objection: the objection, the response, the evidence to
cite); What may not be promised without approval; Hand-over to delivery or
onboarding; Document control; Open questions. Leave out internal discount
limits and margins.
```
