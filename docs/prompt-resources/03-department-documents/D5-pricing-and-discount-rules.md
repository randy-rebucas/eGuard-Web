# D5 Pricing and discount rules (confidential)

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Financial | Confidential | Department: sales and finance (needs the **Retrieve confidential knowledge** authority) |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Pricing and Discount Rules".
Sections: Summary; Price list (table: Product | List price | Currency | Billing
period); Costs and target margins (only if I provided them); Discount limits
(table: Discount size | Who may approve | Conditions); Special terms (payment
terms, multi-year, non-profit, partners); What never gets discounted; How a
price exception is requested and recorded; Document control; Open questions.
Every number must come from my material; anything else is [TO CONFIRM].
```

## After drafting

Upload it as **Confidential**. Only AI employees in the assigned departments that also hold the **Retrieve confidential knowledge** authority can read it. The discount limits in it are not enforced by the document: set the matching approval rules for the employees who quote prices.
