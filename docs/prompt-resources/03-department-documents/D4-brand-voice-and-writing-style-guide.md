# D4 Brand voice and writing style guide

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & marketing | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Brand Voice and Writing Style
Guide". Sections: Summary; Who we write for; Voice (3-4 traits, each with "we
write like this / not like this" examples); Words we use and words we avoid
(table); Formatting conventions (dates, numbers, currency, capitalisation of
product names); Email and message structure; Social media and public posts
(what needs approval before publishing); Claims we may and may not make;
Document control; Open questions. Base the examples on my material; do not
invent slogans.
```
