# D10 People policies summary

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| HR | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "People Policies Summary" from
the HR policies I paste. One "## " section per policy (for example leave,
working hours, remote work, equipment, expenses for staff): who it applies to,
the rule, the numbers (days, amounts, notice periods), how to request, who
approves. Refer to the full policy document by name for details. Leave out
anything about named individuals. Then Document control and Open questions.
```
