# D9 Standard terms summary (confidential)

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Legal | Confidential | Role: the roles that handle contracts |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Standard Contract Terms
Summary" from the contract templates and terms I paste. Sections: Summary;
Contract types we use (one "## " section each: when it is used, term length,
renewal, termination, payment terms, liability cap, data protection clause);
Terms that may be negotiated and by whom; Terms that may never be changed;
How a contract is sent, signed and stored; Document control; Open questions.
Summarise the terms faithfully and quote the exact clause wording where the
detail matters. Do not give legal advice and do not add terms that are not in
my material.
```
