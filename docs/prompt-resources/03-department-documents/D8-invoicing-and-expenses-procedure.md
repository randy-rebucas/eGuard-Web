# D8 Invoicing and expenses procedure

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| SOP (controlled) | Internal | Department: finance |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write the SOP "Invoicing and Expenses
Procedure". Sections: Summary; Issuing an invoice (numbered steps, systems used,
numbering format, payment terms); Following up on unpaid invoices (timeline
table); Recording a payment; Submitting an expense (limits, receipts, who
approves at each amount); Purchases and subscriptions (approval limits);
Month-end close checklist; Document control; Open questions. No bank account
numbers or card details.
```
