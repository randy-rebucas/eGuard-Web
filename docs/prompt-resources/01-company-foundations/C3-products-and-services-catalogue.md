# C3 Products and services catalogue

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & marketing | Internal (Public if it only holds published information) | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Products and Services
Catalogue". One "## " section per product or service, titled with its exact
name. In each section: what it is; who it is for; what is included and not
included; delivery time or availability; public list price with currency
(or [TO CONFIRM]); related products. Do not include costs, margins or
internal discount rules (those go in a separate confidential document).
Then Document control and Open questions.
```
