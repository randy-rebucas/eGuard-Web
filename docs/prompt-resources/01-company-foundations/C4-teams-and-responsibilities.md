# C4 Teams and responsibilities

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Reference | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Teams and Responsibilities".
One "## " section per department: purpose; what it is responsible for; what it is
not responsible for; the roles in it (role titles, not personal details); which
requests it handles and how to reach it (channel or queue name, not personal
phone numbers); who it hands work to and receives work from.
Finish with a table "Who handles what": Request type | Responsible team | Backup.
Describe responsibilities as how the company works; do not describe software
permissions. Then Document control and Open questions.
```
