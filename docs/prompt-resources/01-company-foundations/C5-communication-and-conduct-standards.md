# C5 Communication and conduct standards

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Policy (controlled) | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write the policy "Communication and
Conduct Standards". Sections: Summary; Scope (who the policy applies to);
Tone and professionalism with customers; Promises and commitments (what may not
be promised without approval, and who approves); Accuracy (say what is known,
flag what is not); Handling complaints and upset customers; Escalating to a
person (when and to which role); Prohibited behaviour; Document control;
Open questions. Use "must" and "must not" only for real company rules I gave you.
```
