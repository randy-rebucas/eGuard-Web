# C6 Data handling and confidentiality policy

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Policy (controlled) | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write the policy "Data Handling and
Confidentiality". Sections: Summary; Data categories we hold (customer data,
employee data, financial data, partner data) with examples; Confidentiality
levels used by the company (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) with
examples of each from our business; Who may share what with whom; Rules for
personal data (collection, retention period, deletion); Sending data outside the
company (approved tools and channels only); Reporting a suspected data incident
(to which role, how fast); Document control; Open questions.
Do not cite specific laws unless they are in my fact sheet; mark any legal
requirement I did not give you as [TO CONFIRM].
```
