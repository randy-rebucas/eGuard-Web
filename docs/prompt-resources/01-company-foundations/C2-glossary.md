# C2 Glossary

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Reference | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Company Glossary".
Collect every term, abbreviation, product name, team name, system name and
customer-segment name from the fact sheet and the material I paste.
Group the terms under "## " sections by theme (for example Products, Teams,
Systems, Sales terms). In each section use a table: Term | Meaning | Not to be
confused with. Where two words are used for the same thing, pick the one in the
fact sheet as the official term and list the other under "Not to be confused
with" as an alias. Then Document control and Open questions.
```
