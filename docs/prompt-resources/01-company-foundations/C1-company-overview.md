# C1 Company overview

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Reference | Internal | Company-wide |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "Company Overview".
Sections: Summary; What the company does; Customers and markets; Products and
services at a glance (one line each, then refer to the Products and Services
Catalogue); How the company is organised (departments and what each does);
Working hours, time zone and currency; How work is requested and approved
(in plain terms); Document control; Open questions.
Leave out project-specific details.
```
