# T1 Role lesson pack

| Category | Confidentiality | Available to |
|---|---|---|
| Training | Internal | Role (or attach it to a training program) |

**How to use:** start a new chat and paste, in order, the [house rules](../00-intro/house-rules.md), your [project fact sheet](../00-intro/project-fact-sheet.md), then the prompt below. Paste any existing material after the prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Using the house rules and fact sheet above, write "<Role title>: Lesson Pack".
It teaches a new <role title> how this company does the job. Sections: Summary;
What the role is responsible for (and not responsible for); The five most
important rules for this role, each with the reason; Worked example 1..3
(one "## " section each: the situation, what a good response does step by step,
which document it relies on); Common mistakes and how to avoid them; Where to
find more (names of related documents); Document control; Open questions.
Use only situations and rules from my material.
```
