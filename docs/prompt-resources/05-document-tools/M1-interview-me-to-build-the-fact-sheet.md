# M1 Interview me to build the fact sheet

**How to use:** paste the prompt below into a new chat and fill in the `<paste>` placeholders.

## Prompt

```text
I want to fill in the project fact sheet below, but I would rather be
interviewed. Ask me questions one topic at a time, at most three questions per
message, starting with the company and then the project. When an answer is
vague, ask one follow-up for the specific number, date, name or limit. Do not
suggest answers. When we are done, output the completed fact sheet exactly in
its original format, with "[TO CONFIRM]" for anything I could not answer.

<paste the empty fact sheet here>
```
