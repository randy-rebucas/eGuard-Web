## About eGuard

**What is eGuard?**
A parental control app for Filipino families, made by DevCom Digital Marketing Services in Baybay City, Leyte. You
set rules for your children's devices, such as screen time, bedtime and which apps they can use, and eGuard checks
that those rules are really running on each device.

**How is it different from other parental control apps?**
Most apps stop at "Saved." eGuard sends each setting to the child's device, and the device reports back what it
actually has. Only then is the setting marked **Verified**. If the device reports something else, you see
**Failed** and what the device reported, and you get an alert.

**What is Configuration Health?**
One score per child across 10 protections. It shows how much of what you set up is really in place on their
devices. It measures the devices' settings, never your child's behavior.

## Getting started

**How does eGuard work?**
You create a family account, add your children and their devices, then follow guided steps to switch on the
protections you choose. eGuard keeps checking that each protection is still applied, and alerts you if something
changes.

**How long does setup take?**
A few minutes to pair a child's Android phone: install eGuard on the phone, connect it to your family account, and
switch on the protections you choose.

**Do I need a credit card to start?**
No. The Free plan needs no card.

## Devices

**Which devices does eGuard work on?**
- **Android** phones and tablets, through the eGuard app on Google Play.
- **Chrome, Edge and Firefox**, through the eGuard browser extension.

You manage all of them from one web dashboard.

**Does it work on iPhone and iPad?**
Not yet. The iPhone and iPad app is coming soon. Get an email when it's out at eguard.family/iphone.

**Does it work on Windows PCs?**
Not yet. eGuard for Windows is being tested. It will let you block and limit PC games and apps, like Roblox,
Minecraft and Counter-Strike, from the same dashboard. It never touches a game from the inside, so it's safe with
anti-cheat. There's no release date yet.

**What happens when the phone is offline?**
Protection stays on. Devices keep following the last rules they received, and pick up any changes as soon as they
reconnect, usually within about 5 minutes.

## Rules and protections

**What can I control?**
Daily screen time, bedtime, which apps are allowed, limits on single apps, and age ratings for apps. Plus and
Family Pro add location sharing and category limits, such as one "gaming time" limit across all games.

**What does the browser extension do?**
It blocks harmful sites and categories, turns on SafeSearch, and lets your child ask you before opening a blocked
site.

**Can my child switch it off or uninstall it?**
Uninstall protection keeps eGuard from being removed without you. If a setting is changed on the device anyway, the
device reports what it really has, so eGuard shows it as Failed and sends you an alert naming the device and the
setting.

**How will I know if something stops working?**
Every plan gets email and in-app alerts. Plus and Family Pro also get push alerts in the eGuard parent app.

## Privacy

**Will my child know eGuard is on?**
Yes. eGuard is never hidden. Your child can always open it and see which rules are on and how much screen time is
left. On Android it also shows a notification while it's active. There's a page that explains eGuard to children
at eguard.family/for-kids.

**What can I see, and what stays private?**
You see whether each protection is on, screen-time totals, the names of apps used, and location only if your family
turns it on. eGuard never sees messages, photos, calls, what your child types, or which websites they visit, beyond
a site they ask you to unblock.

**How is my family's data protected?**
eGuard collects only what it needs to verify your protections. It shows no ads and never sells your children's
data. You can export or delete your family's data at any time from Settings. Our privacy policy is written under
the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), and is at eguard.family/privacy.

## Plans and payment

**How much does eGuard cost?**

| **Free** | ₱0 | 1 | Protection setup, screen time, limited app monitoring |
| **eGuard Plus** | ₱149 / month or ₱1,490 / year | Up to 5 | Everything, plus location sharing, category limits and push alerts |
| **Family Pro** | ₱249 / month or ₱2,490 / year | Up to 10 | All of Plus, plus advanced reports and API access for schools and organizations |

**Is verification included on the Free plan?**
Yes. Every plan checks each protection on the device and shows you Configuration Health. Paying adds capacity and
features, not honesty.

**Is paying yearly cheaper?**
Yes. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months: two months free.

**How can I pay?**
GCash, Maya, QR Ph or a card.

**What's the difference between a pass and auto-renew?**
A pass is one month or one year, paid once. It never renews. We remind you 3 days before it ends, and buying
another adds to it. Auto-renew charges a card or Maya every month or every year until you turn it off.

**Can I pay in the app?**
Plans are bought on the web, in Settings › Subscription. Only the family admin can buy or cancel. The app shows your
plan as soon as it's paid.

**What happens if I move to a smaller plan?**
Nothing is removed. Children and devices over the new limit stay protected; you just can't add more until you're
under it. If location sharing ends, current locations are cleared and any history is hidden until you upgrade
again.

## Schools and organizations

**Can schools or organizations use eGuard?**
Yes. Any parent can create an organization for a school, community group or business, share a join code with
families, and buy sponsor codes that pay for families' plans. Family Pro adds API keys, so your own systems can
read your codes and counts. Organizations only ever see counts, never a family's data. See eguard.family/for-schools.
